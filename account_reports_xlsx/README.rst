Accounting Reports In Excel
===========================
Generates XLSX reports for all accounting records of a business.

Key Features
------------
* Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss reports in Excel (.xlsx) format.
* General Ledger report with initial balances and running balances.
* Partner Ledger for receivables and payables with multi-currency details.
* Aged Partner Balance with configurable aging intervals.
* Fast in-memory Excel streaming with professional formatting.

Configuration
=============
* No additional configuration is needed.
